Nail Salon Prices UK 2026: Expert Guide to Costs & Trends

14 October 2026

Wondering how much to budget for your next set of nails? With the cost of living rising and new trends emerging, knowing the real nail salon prices UK 2026 can help you plan whether it's a treat or a regular self-care ritual. This guide gives you honest, up-to-date price comparisons and tips for every budget.

Quick answer: The average nail salon prices UK 2026 range from £18–£30 for a standard manicure, £25–£45 for a pedicure, and £30–£60 for gel nails. Nail art, extensions, and luxury treatments cost more. Prices vary by location and salon type across the United Kingdom.

Key Takeaways

  • Manicure costs in the UK typically fall between £18 and £30 in 2026, with higher rates in London and city centres.
  • Pedicures cost £25–£45 on average, depending on treatment type and salon location.
  • Nail art pricing UK 2026 starts from £5 extra, but complex designs can add £15–£30 to your bill.
  • Luxury nail salons and branded chains charge significantly more than independent or local options.
  • Look for nail salon discounts UK 2026 or off-peak deals for budget-friendly treatments.

Nail Salon Prices UK 2026: What to Expect

Average Manicure and Pedicure Costs

The average manicure cost in the UK in 2026 sits at £18–£30 for a standard service, with prices at the lower end in smaller towns like Shrewsbury or Lincoln (£18–£22) and rising to £28–£35 in London or Manchester. A basic pedicure costs £25–£45, influenced by the salon’s setting, products, and experience. Luxury nail services UK—think high-end spas or designer salons in Knightsbridge—can charge £50+ for signature manicures.

Nail Extensions, Gel, and Acrylics

Gel and acrylic nail extensions remain popular. Expect to pay £30–£60 for a full set, with infills costing £22–£35. Some affordable nail salons in the UK offer express gel polish from £20, but branded systems (like BIAB or CND Shellac) usually sit at £35–£45. Removal and repair may cost extra, typically £7–£15.

Understanding Nail Art Pricing and Add-Ons

How Much Does Nail Art Cost in 2026?

Nail art pricing UK 2026 varies widely. Simple designs (dots, stripes, accent nails) start from £5–£7 extra, while intricate or bespoke art can add £15–£30, depending on the artist’s skill and time required. Chrome, foils, and embellishments are often priced per nail. Always ask for a quote—prices should be made clear before booking, as advised by Which?.

Other Common Extras

Popular add-ons in UK salons include French tips (£5–£10), hand/foot masks (£5–£8), or strengthening treatments (£3–£7). Removal of previous gel or acrylics may cost £7–£15, especially if you didn’t have your last set done at the same salon. Always check the full nail treatment prices UK before your appointment.

Nail Salon Price Comparison UK: Budget vs Luxury

Budget-Friendly Nail Salons UK

Looking for budget-friendly nail salons UK? Local independents in places like Sheffield, Swansea, or Coventry often offer basic manicures from £18–£22 and simple gel nails from £25. Some chains, like Nails Inc Express Bars, offer off-peak rates or student discounts, helping you save on nail salon cost UK 2026.

Luxury Nail Services UK

Premium salons in cities such as London, Edinburgh, and Bath may charge £40–£60 for a classic manicure. These venues often use luxury products, offer extensive aftercare, and include extras like massage or champagne. Expect bespoke nail art or sculpted extensions to start at £55 and climb higher, particularly in central London where nail salon fees London 2026 are highest in the United Kingdom.

Nail Care Pricing Trends 2026

Why Are Prices Changing?

In 2026, nail care pricing trends reflect increased costs for supplies, staff wages, and business rates, especially in city centres. The move towards eco-friendly products and advanced nail systems (like BIAB, vegan gels, or LED curing) also raises costs. However, the United Kingdom’s competitive market means many salons run promotions or loyalty schemes to attract regulars.

Where to Find Cheap Nail Salons UK

To find where to find cheap nail salons UK, look for independent nail bars outside major city centres, check community Facebook groups, or use booking apps that display prices upfront. Many salons offer discounts for first-time clients, students, or NHS workers. Off-peak appointments (weekday mornings) can be more affordable, especially in areas like Leeds or Bristol. Price transparency is regulated by the Competition and Markets Authority, so salons must display clear pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do pedicures cost UK in 2026?

Standard pedicures cost £25–£45 in the UK, with higher prices in London and premium salons. Add-ons such as gel polish or spa treatments will increase the total.

Is nail art included in manicure prices?

Usually not. Most salons charge extra for nail art, ranging from £5 for simple designs to £30+ for detailed work. Always ask for a price breakdown before booking.

Are there nail salon discounts UK 2026?

Yes, many UK salons offer discounts for students, NHS staff, or off-peak appointments. Check salon websites or booking apps for current offers and seasonal deals.

What affects nail salon cost UK 2026?

Location, salon reputation, product quality, and the complexity of treatments all influence costs. Central city salons and luxury chains usually charge more than independent local options.

How can I ensure I’m paying a fair price?

Compare quotes, check reviews, and look for transparent price lists. UK salons must follow consumer law for pricing clarity—see gov.uk and Which? for your rights as a client.
