Cost of Hair Extensions in Manchester

1 November 2026

Thinking about transforming your look with longer, fuller hair? Many Manchester residents are drawn to hair extensions, but the cost of hair extensions in Manchester can be confusing—and sometimes a shock. With so many options, salons, and hidden extras, it’s easy to feel overwhelmed when budgeting for this beauty investment.

Quick answer: The cost of hair extensions in Manchester in 2026 starts at £120 for basic clip-ins, with professional salon-applied extensions averaging £250–£450 for a full head. Premium and luxury options can exceed £1,000, depending on hair quality, method, and ongoing maintenance requirements.

Key Takeaways

  • Hair extension prices Manchester range from £120 for clip-ins to over £1,000 for luxury installations in top salons.

  • Type, method, hair quality, and stylist expertise all affect the cost of hair extensions in Manchester.

  • Ongoing maintenance for semi-permanent extensions can add £40–£120 every 6–8 weeks.

  • Choosing reputable salons helps ensure quality and proper aftercare, essential for protecting your natural hair.

  • The United Kingdom offers a wide variety of hair extension services to fit every budget and occasion.

Breaking Down the Cost of Hair Extensions in Manchester

Understanding Price Ranges Across Extension Types

The cost of hair extensions in Manchester is shaped by your choice of extension type. Temporary clip-in hair extensions price Manchester shoppers can expect is £45–£120 for a full set, while professional salon-applied options such as tape-ins, micro-rings, weaves, and bonds range from £180–£1,200 depending on the quality and volume of hair. Most people seeking a noticeable transformation will pay between £250 and £450 for standard, semi-permanent installations using good-quality Remy human hair. For those desiring premium Russian or European hair, or especially thick or long extensions, costs can easily reach or surpass £1,000 in Manchester’s luxury salons.

Key Factors Affecting Hair Extension Costs

A number of factors affect hair extension costs in Manchester. The main ones are:

  • Hair quality: Remy, double-drawn, or ethically sourced hair commands higher prices.

  • Length and thickness: Longer and fuller sets require more hair and labour, increasing the cost.

  • Application method: Clip-ins are cheapest; professional bonded, taped, or weaved extensions require skilled fitting and carry higher charges.

  • Stylist expertise: Leading Manchester hair extension services and stylists charge more for their experience and reputation.

  • Salon location and facilities: City centre or boutique salons may have higher overheads reflected in their prices.

How Much Does Hair Extension Maintenance Cost in Manchester?

Salon Maintenance and Upkeep

The initial cost of hair extensions in Manchester is only part of the picture. Professional semi-permanent extensions need regular maintenance every 6–8 weeks. Manchester hair extension salons typically charge £40–£120 per session for tightening, refitting, or re-taping. Leaving extensions too long risks matting, tangling, or hair damage—so maintenance is non-negotiable for long-term wearers. The most affordable hair extensions Manchester offers (clip-ins and halos) require little professional upkeep, only basic home care and occasional replacement.

Long-Term Costs and Removal

Aftercare adds up over time. For example, a full head of micro-rings costing £350 might require four maintenance appointments per year (£80 each), plus £100 for removal at the end of their life. That’s over £700 in total for one year. Luxury hair extensions Manchester clients choose may have even higher annual costs due to specialist aftercare products and more frequent salon visits. Always ask about removal fees and aftercare products, as some salons charge extra, while others include these in their premium packages.

Where to Buy Hair Extensions in Manchester and the UK

Salons, Specialist Shops, and Online Options

There’s no shortage of places to buy hair extensions in Manchester. City centre salons, independent stylists, and specialist beauty shops all stock a variety of brands, hair types, and price points. For personalised advice, colour matching, and fitting, Manchester hair extension services in established salons are often best—especially for first-timers or those investing in premium options. Online retailers and high street shops can offer savings on temporary and DIY extensions, but quality and aftercare vary, so read reviews and check return policies before buying.

What to Look For in a Supplier

When deciding where to buy hair extensions in Manchester, prioritise suppliers who offer clear information on hair origin, ethical sourcing, and care advice. Look for stylists and salons registered with UK trade bodies, or those listed by Trading Standards. Reputable salons should provide a thorough consultation, accurate quotes, and written aftercare instructions. Questions to Ask Before Booking

  • What is included in the quoted cost of hair extensions in Manchester—hair, fitting, cut, aftercare products?

  • Are maintenance and removal included, or charged separately?

  • Is the hair guaranteed for a certain period or number of refits?

  • What aftercare is recommended for my hair type and extension method?

  • Is the stylist trained and insured, and do they follow UK regulations?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do hair extensions cost in Manchester’s top salons?

In Manchester, a full head of salon-applied extensions costs £250–£450 on average, with luxury options in leading salons reaching £1,000 or more. Prices depend on hair type, length, and stylist reputation.

Can I get affordable hair extensions Manchester-wide without compromising on quality?

Yes, especially with temporary options or by choosing reputable stylists who use good-quality but not premium hair. Always check reviews and ask about aftercare and guarantees for the best value.

What are the main factors affecting hair extension costs?

Hair quality, length, volume, application method, stylist experience, and required maintenance all impact the cost of hair extensions in Manchester and the wider UK.

How often do I need to maintain hair extensions?

Semi-permanent extensions require maintenance every 6–8 weeks at a cost of £40–£120 per session in Manchester. Clip-ins and halos need no salon upkeep, just home care.

Are there regulations for hair extension salons in the United Kingdom?

Yes, reputable salons should comply with UK health, safety, and Trading Standards guidelines, including insurance and ethical hair sourcing. You can check for complaints or certifications with your local Trading Standards office or Which? (2026).
